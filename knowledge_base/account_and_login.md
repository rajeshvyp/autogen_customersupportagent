# Account, Login and Password Support
Version: 1.2 | Owner: Customer Support

## Password reset
1. Select the password-reset option on the sign-in page.
2. Enter the email address associated with the account.
3. Follow the reset link sent to that address.
4. Create a unique password that has not been reused elsewhere.
5. Return to the sign-in page and log in.

## Reset email not received
Check spam/junk folders, confirm the email address and wait a few minutes before requesting another link. If the account email may be incorrect, refer to support for identity verification.

## Account locked
Repeated unsuccessful login attempts may temporarily restrict access. Do not advise customers to bypass security controls. Refer persistent lockouts to Account Support.

## Change email address
Account email changes require verification through the approved account process. Do not change account ownership based only on an email request.

## Multi-factor authentication
Never ask a customer to disclose a one-time code or recovery code. Guide them to the official account security flow.

## Suspected account compromise
Treat unexpected password-reset messages, unknown orders or changed account details as high priority. Route to Trust & Safety and advise the customer to secure their email account through its provider.

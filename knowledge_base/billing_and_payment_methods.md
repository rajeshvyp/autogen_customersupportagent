# Billing and Payment Methods
Version: 1.0 | Owner: Finance

## Accepted payment methods
Available methods are shown at checkout and may vary by destination. The agent must not claim a payment method is accepted unless checkout or the current payment configuration confirms it.

## Payment declined
The retailer may not receive a detailed decline reason. Ask the customer to check the payment details with their provider and try an available alternative. Never ask for full card details or security codes.

## Invoice
Customers should provide order ID and billing name. Finance can review invoice corrections. Do not alter tax details without verification.

## Currency and taxes
The checkout page displays the applicable currency, taxes and delivery charges before order confirmation. Cross-border tax treatment may vary.

## Payment security
Never request passwords, full card number, CVV, one-time passcodes or bank login information.

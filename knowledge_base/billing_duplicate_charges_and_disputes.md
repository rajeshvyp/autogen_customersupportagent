# Duplicate Charges, Pending Payments and Disputes
Version: 1.0 | Owner: Finance

## Duplicate charge report
Treat multiple apparent completed charges as high priority. Collect order ID, transaction dates, amounts and payment method type. Do not request full card details.

## Pending authorisation
A pending authorisation may temporarily reduce available funds but may not be a completed capture. Finance must verify the transaction state.

## Payment dispute
If the customer reports a chargeback, bank dispute, suspected fraud or an unauthorised transaction, escalate to Finance immediately.

## Refund versus reversal
A refund is submitted after a transaction has been captured. An authorisation reversal releases a pending hold. These are different processes.

## Customer wording
Acknowledge the concern, explain that Finance will review the transaction records and avoid promising a result before reconciliation.

# Business Orders, Quotes and Invoices
Version: 1.0 | Owner: Business Sales

## Business enquiries
Collect company name, business contact, product list, quantities, delivery location and required date. Do not promise bulk stock or a discount before confirmation.

## Quotes
A quote is not an order confirmation. Pricing and availability must be confirmed by Business Sales.

## Purchase orders
If a customer asks to pay by purchase order, refer to Business Sales for account eligibility and terms.

## Tax and invoice corrections
Request the order ID and the exact field requiring correction. Finance verifies changes before issuing a revised document.

## Large orders
Orders for organisations, schools or public bodies may require a separate quotation and delivery plan.

# TechnoHexagone Store — Company Overview
Version: 1.0 | Owner: Customer Experience | Effective: 1 January 2026

## About the company
TechnoHexagone Store is a fictional omnichannel electronics retailer serving individual consumers and small businesses. It sells laptops, smartphones, tablets, monitors, networking equipment, printers, audio devices, smart-home products and accessories. It operates an online store and a central customer-support team.

## Customer support principles
1. Verify the customer's order or device details before discussing account-specific information.
2. Use the policies in this knowledge base; do not promise an exception unless an authorised team has approved it.
3. Explain the next step, expected timeframe and any information the customer must provide.
4. Never request a full payment-card number, password, one-time passcode or account recovery code.
5. Escalate safety, fraud, legal, payment-dispute and unresolved high-impact cases.

## Departments
- Customer Support: first-line questions, order status and basic troubleshooting.
- Logistics: carrier investigations, lost parcels and delivery exceptions.
- Returns & Refunds: return authorisations, refunds and cancellations.
- Technical Service: diagnostics, warranty assessment and repairs.
- Finance: payment reconciliation, duplicate charges and invoices.
- Trust & Safety: suspected account compromise and fraud.

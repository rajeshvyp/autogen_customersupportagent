# Escalation Matrix
Version: 1.0 | Owner: Customer Experience

## High-priority escalation
Escalate when urgency is High, customer sentiment indicates severe anger or distress, the knowledge base has no relevant evidence, confidence is low, or the issue involves safety, suspected fraud, payment disputes or legal threats.

## Routing
- Delivery investigation, missing parcel, carrier dispute → Logistics.
- Return eligibility, damaged goods, refund status → Returns & Refunds.
- Device fault, repair, safety issue → Technical Service.
- Duplicate charge, chargeback, payment reconciliation → Finance.
- Account compromise, privacy incident, phishing → Trust & Safety.
- Bulk order, quote, purchase order → Business Sales.

## Minimum handoff
Include ticket ID, concise summary, impact, order/product identifiers if supplied, customer sentiment, steps attempted, retrieved sources and escalation reason.

## Agent restrictions
The AI may recommend an escalation and prepare a handoff. It must not claim that a human has accepted the case unless the system confirms assignment.

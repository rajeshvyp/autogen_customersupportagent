# Customer FAQ — Accounts and Billing

## I forgot my password. What should I do?
Use the password-reset option on the sign-in page and follow the link sent to your registered email.

## I did not receive the reset email.
Check spam/junk, confirm the email address and wait a few minutes. Contact support if the problem continues.

## Why was my payment declined?
The payment provider may not share the exact reason. Check with the provider or try another method shown at checkout.

## I see two charges.
A pending authorisation may look different from a completed charge. Multiple completed charges must be reviewed by Finance.

## Can I send you my card details?
No. Never send full card numbers, CVV or one-time codes to support.

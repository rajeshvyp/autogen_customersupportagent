# Customer FAQ — Orders and Delivery

## How can I track my order?
Use the tracking link sent after dispatch. Initial carrier scans can take up to 24 hours to appear.

## Why has tracking not updated?
Carrier scans may be delayed. If there has been no movement for 3 business days, support can request a Logistics investigation.

## Can I change my delivery address?
A change may be possible before dispatch. After dispatch it cannot be guaranteed.

## Why did my order arrive in two parcels?
Some items may ship from different warehouses. Check all tracking references associated with the order.

## Tracking says delivered, but I have nothing.
Check safe places, reception and household members. Then provide the order ID so Logistics can investigate.

## Can you guarantee delivery tomorrow?
No. Delivery estimates are not guarantees. Check the current carrier estimate.

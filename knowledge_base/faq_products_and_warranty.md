# Customer FAQ — Products and Warranty

## Is every USB-C charger compatible?
No. Connector shape alone does not establish charging compatibility. Check the exact device requirements.

## My laptop is overheating. Should I keep using it?
If there is smoke, burning smell, swelling, sparks or excessive heat, stop using it and escalate. Otherwise, keep vents clear and use a hard, flat surface.

## How long is the warranty?
Eligible TechnoHexagone-branded products have a 24-month limited commercial warranty. Third-party products follow their stated terms. Statutory rights may also apply.

## Can I upgrade memory?
It depends on the exact model and configuration. Check the product specification or refer to Technical Service.

## Will repair erase my data?
Service may involve resetting or replacing storage. Back up personal data where possible before service.

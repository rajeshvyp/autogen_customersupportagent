# Customer FAQ — Returns and Refunds

## How long do I have to return an item?
The general return window is 30 calendar days from delivery for eligible products, subject to exclusions and applicable law.

## Do I need the original packaging?
Secure packaging is important. Include supplied accessories and documentation. Contact support if packaging is unavailable.

## When will I receive my refund?
After an approved return is received and inspected, the refund is submitted to the original payment method. The provider may take additional time to display it.

## Can I exchange an item?
An exchange depends on stock and approval. Returns & Refunds can confirm available options.

## My product arrived damaged. What should I do?
Stop using it if it may be unsafe. Keep packaging and provide photographs. Support will route the case for assessment.

## Can I return an opened product?
Eligibility depends on product type, condition, exclusions and applicable law. Provide the product and order details for review.

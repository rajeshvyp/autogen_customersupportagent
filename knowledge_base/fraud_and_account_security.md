# Fraud and Account Security Escalation
Version: 1.0 | Owner: Trust & Safety

## Indicators
- Customer reports an order they did not place.
- Account email or password changed without permission.
- Customer receives unexpected authentication codes.
- Payment method is used without authorisation.
- Customer reports phishing or impersonation.

## Immediate actions
1. Mark the ticket High priority.
2. Do not ask for passwords or authentication codes.
3. Avoid confirming sensitive account details until verification.
4. Route to Trust & Safety.
5. Preserve relevant ticket references and timestamps.

## Phishing
Tell customers not to click suspicious links or share credentials. They should use the official website or app reached independently.

## Fraud claims
Do not accuse a person of fraud or determine liability. Escalate for investigation.

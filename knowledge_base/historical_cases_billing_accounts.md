# Historical Support Cases — Billing and Account Security
Dataset: fictional examples for retrieval and testing

## Case BA-4001 — Duplicate completed charges
Customer issue: Customer reported two completed charges for one order.
Resolution: Marked high priority and routed to Finance with transaction dates and order reference.
Sources: billing_duplicate_charges_and_disputes.md
Outcome: Escalated to Finance.

## Case BA-4002 — Password reset email missing
Customer issue: Reset email not received.
Resolution: Suggested checking spam/junk, verifying registered address and waiting before retrying.
Sources: account_and_login.md
Outcome: Resolved.

## Case BA-4003 — Unknown order in account
Customer issue: Customer saw an order they did not place.
Resolution: Did not disclose further account details; routed to Trust & Safety and advised securing email account.
Sources: fraud_and_account_security.md; privacy_and_data_protection.md
Outcome: High-priority escalation.

## Case BA-4004 — Customer offered a one-time code
Customer issue: Customer pasted an authentication code into the support message.
Resolution: Agent must not repeat or use the code; advised customer never to share codes and routed any compromise concern to Trust & Safety.
Sources: privacy_and_data_protection.md; fraud_and_account_security.md
Outcome: Security guidance and review.

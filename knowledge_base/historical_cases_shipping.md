# Historical Support Cases — Shipping
Dataset: fictional examples for retrieval and testing

## Case SH-1001 — Tracking not updated
Customer issue: Tracking showed label created for two days.
Resolution: Explained that the carrier may not have scanned the parcel yet; requested follow-up if no movement after the investigation threshold.
Sources: shipping_policy.md; shipping_exceptions_and_carriers.md
Outcome: Resolved with monitoring.

## Case SH-1002 — Marked delivered but not received
Customer issue: Carrier status showed delivered; customer could not locate parcel.
Resolution: Asked customer to check safe places and reception, then routed to Logistics for delivery confirmation investigation.
Sources: shipping_policy.md; shipping_exceptions_and_carriers.md
Outcome: Escalated to Logistics.

## Case SH-1003 — Two parcels for one order

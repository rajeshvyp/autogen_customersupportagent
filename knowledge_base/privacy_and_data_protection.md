# Privacy and Customer Data Handling
Version: 1.0 | Owner: Trust & Safety

## Data minimisation
Collect only information needed to identify the order and resolve the issue. Avoid copying unnecessary personal information into ticket notes.

## Prohibited requests
Never request account passwords, full payment-card numbers, CVV, one-time passcodes, private encryption keys or recovery codes.

## Identity verification
Use the approved account verification workflow before disclosing order details, changing account information or discussing sensitive records.

## Data access and deletion requests
Route formal access, correction or deletion requests to the privacy team. Do not promise deletion before the request is verified and processed.

## Suspected data exposure
Escalate immediately to Trust & Safety. Record what is known, when it was discovered and the systems or information potentially involved. Do not speculate about the scope.

## Third-party information
Do not disclose another customer's personal data, order details or contact information.

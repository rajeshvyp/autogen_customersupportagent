# Product Catalogue — Audio and Smart Home
Version: 1.0 | Owner: Product Team

All products below are fictional training examples.

## TN Buds Air
- Category: Wireless earbuds
- Features: Bluetooth pairing, charging case and touch controls
- Common questions: one earbud not charging, pairing reset and microphone quality

## TN SoundBar 2
- Category: Soundbar
- Inputs: HDMI ARC, optical and Bluetooth
- Common questions: TV audio settings and remote pairing

## TN HomeCam Indoor
- Category: Indoor security camera
- Features: Wi-Fi connection, motion alerts and local storage support
- Privacy: customer must follow local recording and privacy laws

## TN Smart Plug
- Category: Smart-home accessory
- Features: app control and scheduling
- Common questions: Wi-Fi band compatibility and factory reset

## Safety and privacy
Do not advise customers to bypass electrical safety protections or disable security controls. Escalate suspected unauthorised access to Trust & Safety.

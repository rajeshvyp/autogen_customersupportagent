# Returns and Refund Policy
Version: 2.0 | Owner: Returns & Refunds | Effective: 1 January 2026

## Return eligibility
Unused products in their original condition and packaging may generally be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery, subject to product-specific exclusions and applicable consumer law.

## Return request
The customer should provide the order ID, item name, reason for return and whether the product has been opened or used. Support or the returns portal provides the return instructions and authorisation where required.

## Product condition
Products should include supplied accessories and documentation. Missing parts, physical damage or signs of use may affect the assessment. Do not decide the final refund amount before inspection where inspection is required.

## Refund process
After an approved return is received and inspected, the refund is submitted to the original payment method. The bank or payment provider may require additional time to display the credit. Do not guarantee an exact posting date.

## Damaged or faulty item
Ask for a description and relevant photographs. Do not require a customer to continue using a product that may be unsafe. Route safety concerns to Technical Service immediately.

## Non-returnable or restricted items
Personalised items, opened hygiene-sensitive products and certain software or digital products may have restrictions. Check the product-specific terms and applicable law before advising.

## Exchanges
An exchange depends on stock availability. If the requested replacement is unavailable, Returns & Refunds will explain available options.

## Refund status
If the customer says a refund has not appeared, request the return/order reference and refund confirmation date. Refer cases beyond the stated processing status to Finance or Returns & Refunds.

## Important
This document is a fictional training policy. Where consumer law gives the customer stronger rights, applicable law takes precedence. Escalate legal or disputed entitlement questions to a human.

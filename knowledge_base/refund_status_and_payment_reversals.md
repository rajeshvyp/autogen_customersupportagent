# Refund Status, Reversals and Payment Questions
Version: 1.0 | Owner: Finance

## Refund stages
- Requested: customer has asked for a refund.
- Awaiting return: item has not yet reached the returns facility.
- Under inspection: returned item is being assessed.
- Approved: refund has been authorised.
- Submitted: refund instruction has been sent to the payment provider.
- Completed by provider: the provider reports completion; bank display timing may vary.

## Refund not visible
Ask for order ID, refund reference if available, approval date and payment method type (never request full card details). Check the recorded stage before advising.

## Reversal or pending authorisation
A pending card authorisation is not necessarily a completed charge. Finance must verify whether it was captured, reversed or remains pending.

## Duplicate charge
Treat a report of multiple completed charges as high priority. Do not tell the customer to wait without checking the transaction records. Escalate to Finance.

## Chargeback or bank dispute
If the customer has opened or intends to open a payment dispute, route to Finance immediately. Do not discourage the customer from contacting their bank and do not make legal claims.

## Sensitive data
Never request a full card number, security code, banking password or one-time passcode.

# Repairs, Replacements and Service Centre Process
Version: 1.0 | Owner: Technical Service

## Initial triage
Collect product model, serial number where available, order ID, symptoms, when the problem began and troubleshooting already attempted.

## Repair authorisation
A repair must be authorised before the customer sends a product to a service centre. The agent must not invent a repair address or issue an unauthorised shipping instruction.

## Repair timeline
The service centre provides an estimate after receiving and assessing the device. Actual time depends on parts availability, fault complexity and workload.

## Replacement
Replacement may be considered after assessment under warranty or returns policy. Stock availability and approval must be confirmed by the responsible team.

## Data backup
Customers should back up personal data before service where possible. Service may involve resetting or replacing storage. Never ask the customer to share passwords or recovery codes.

## Repair status
Use the service reference to check received, diagnosis, awaiting parts, repair in progress, quality check or dispatch status.

## Unsuccessful repair
If the same fault remains after service, reopen the case and attach the previous service reference and outcome.

# Customer Communication Tone and Templates
Version: 1.0 | Owner: Customer Experience

## Tone
Be calm, respectful, clear and concise. Acknowledge the customer's experience without admitting facts that have not been verified. Avoid blame, jargon and exaggerated promises.

## Suggested structure
1. Greeting.
2. Acknowledge the reported issue.
3. State what the available policy or knowledge base confirms.
4. Explain the next step and information needed.
5. Give a realistic timeframe only when documented.
6. Close politely.

## Delay acknowledgement
"Thank you for letting us know. I understand that the delay is frustrating. The latest tracking information will help our Logistics team review the shipment."

## Missing information
"I can help check this, but I need your order ID and the delivery postcode to locate the correct record."

## No knowledge-base match
"I don't have enough verified information to answer that accurately. I will refer this question to the appropriate support team for review."

## Safety issue
"Please stop using the device if you notice smoke, swelling, sparks or a burning smell. We will refer this to our Technical Service team for safe next steps."

## Prohibited wording
Do not say "guaranteed tomorrow", "your refund will arrive tomorrow", "this is definitely covered" or "we have assigned an agent" unless the relevant system or policy confirms it.

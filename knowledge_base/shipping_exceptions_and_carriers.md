# Carrier Exceptions and Delivery Investigations
Version: 1.0 | Owner: Logistics

## Common tracking statuses
- Label created: shipment data exists; carrier may not yet have the parcel.
- In transit: parcel is moving through the carrier network.
- Exception: carrier reports an event requiring attention.
- Out for delivery: carrier intends to deliver that day, but this is not a guarantee.
- Delivered: carrier reports delivery; disputes require investigation.
- Return to sender: carrier is returning the parcel to the sender.

## Investigation triggers
Open a Logistics investigation when tracking has no movement for 3 business days, a parcel is marked delivered but not received, a parcel is returned unexpectedly, or the delivery address appears incorrect.

## Information required
Order ID, tracking number, delivery postcode, date of last tracking event and a short description of what the customer has checked.

## Customer communication
Explain what is known and what remains unconfirmed. Do not say a parcel is lost until Logistics or the carrier investigation confirms it.

## Weather and disruption
Severe weather, strikes, customs checks and peak-season volume can affect delivery. Share only disruption information confirmed by the carrier or Logistics team.

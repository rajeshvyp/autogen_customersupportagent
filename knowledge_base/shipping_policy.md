# Shipping and Delivery Policy
Version: 2.1 | Owner: Logistics | Effective: 1 January 2026

## Standard delivery
Standard delivery normally takes 3–5 business days after dispatch. Order processing normally takes up to 1 business day, excluding public holidays and exceptional operational disruptions.

## Express delivery
Eligible destinations may offer express delivery, normally 1–2 business days after dispatch. Availability and fees are displayed at checkout. Express service is not available for every product or postcode.

## Tracking
A tracking link is sent after the carrier accepts the parcel. Tracking may take up to 24 hours to show its first scan. Customers should check the tracking link before reporting a parcel as missing.

## Delayed delivery
If the parcel is beyond the carrier's estimated delivery date, support should:
1. Confirm the order ID and delivery postcode.
2. Check the latest tracking event.
3. Explain that carrier estimates can change.
4. If there is no movement for 3 business days, open a carrier investigation.
5. If the parcel is more than 7 business days beyond the expected date, refer it to Logistics for a formal review.

## Missing parcel
A customer should report a parcel as missing when tracking says delivered but it cannot be located, or when tracking has stopped updating. Ask the customer to check safe places, reception and household members where appropriate. Do not close the case solely because the carrier marked it delivered. Refer disputed delivery confirmation to Logistics.

## Address changes
An address correction may be requested before dispatch. After dispatch, an amendment cannot be guaranteed. Never promise that a carrier will redirect a parcel.

## Partial shipments
An order may arrive in multiple parcels if items ship from different warehouses. Check each tracking number before treating an order as incomplete.

## Damaged parcel on arrival
Ask the customer to retain the packaging and provide photographs of the outer box, shipping label and damaged item. Route the case to Returns & Refunds or Logistics depending on the issue.

## International delivery
Customs processing, duties and local carrier handoffs can extend delivery times. The customer is responsible for any duties clearly disclosed at checkout. Refer disputed or unclear customs charges to Logistics.

## Exclusions
Delivery estimates are not guarantees. The support agent must not invent a carrier scan, delivery date or compensation entitlement.

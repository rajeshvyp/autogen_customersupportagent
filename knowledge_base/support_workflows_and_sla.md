# Support Workflows and Service Targets
Version: 1.0 | Owner: Customer Experience

## Ticket acknowledgement
The system should acknowledge receipt immediately. Acknowledgement is not a promise that the issue is resolved.

## Internal response targets
- Low priority: first human response within 2 business days.
- Medium priority: first human response within 1 business day.
- High priority: route to the priority queue for review as soon as possible during support hours.
- Suspected fraud, account takeover, safety risk or legal threat: immediate human review.

These are internal targets, not guaranteed resolution times.

## Ticket lifecycle
New → Classified → Knowledge Review → Drafted → Quality Check → Resolved or Escalated.
A ticket can be reopened if the customer reports that the proposed solution did not work.

## Required ticket fields
Ticket ID, received timestamp, customer name if supplied, order ID if supplied, complaint text, category, urgency, sentiment, retrieved source files, response, confidence, status and escalation reason.

## Escalation handoff
Include a concise issue summary, customer impact, relevant order/device identifiers, actions already attempted, source documents consulted and the reason human judgement is required.

## No unsupported promises
Do not guarantee carrier delivery dates, refund arrival dates, repair outcomes or warranty coverage before the responsible team confirms them.

# Troubleshooting Guide — Audio and Smart Home
Version: 1.0 | Owner: Technical Service

## Earbud has no sound on one side
Check charge level, clean only according to the manual and repeat the supported pairing reset. Do not use sharp objects or liquids in the speaker mesh.

## Bluetooth pairing fails
Remove stale pairings, place devices within the recommended range and confirm pairing mode. Exact steps depend on the model.

## Soundbar has no TV audio
Confirm the selected input, HDMI ARC/eARC compatibility or optical connection, and the TV audio output setting.

## Smart plug offline
Check Wi-Fi band compatibility, signal strength, app permissions and device status. Do not advise bypassing electrical protection.

## Camera privacy concern
Explain the documented privacy controls and account security steps. Escalate suspected unauthorised viewing or account access to Trust & Safety.

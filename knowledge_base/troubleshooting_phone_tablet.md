# Troubleshooting Guide — Phones and Tablets
Version: 1.0 | Owner: Technical Service

## Device will not charge
- Check the outlet and compatible charging cable.
- Inspect the port for visible debris without inserting metal objects.
- Try a known-compatible charger if safe.
- Stop if the device becomes unusually hot, smells burnt or shows battery swelling.

## Cannot connect to Wi-Fi
- Confirm whether other devices connect to the same network.
- Restart the router and device if safe.
- Forget and rejoin the network.
- Check whether the router uses a supported security mode.

## App crashes
Record the app name, version, operating system version and whether the problem occurs after a specific action. Try an app update before reinstalling, and warn that local app data may be removed.

## Data transfer
Use the manufacturer's supported transfer method. Never ask customers to provide account passwords or one-time codes to support.

## Screen damage
Ask whether the screen is cracked, unresponsive or showing display artefacts. Physical damage may not be covered by the commercial warranty; assessment is required.

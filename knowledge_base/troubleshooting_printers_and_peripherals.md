# Troubleshooting Guide — Printers and Peripherals
Version: 1.0 | Owner: Technical Service

## Printer not detected
Confirm power, connection type, network and device operating system. Check that the correct printer is selected and that the manufacturer-supported driver is installed.

## Paper jam
Follow the printer's manual. Turn the printer off before clearing accessible paper paths if the manual instructs this. Do not force rollers or dismantle the printer.

## Poor print quality
Check ink/toner levels, paper type and the manufacturer's cleaning procedure. Avoid repeated cleaning cycles if the manual warns they consume supplies.

## Keyboard or mouse not connecting
Check battery, receiver, Bluetooth pairing and compatibility. Test another port where relevant.

## Peripheral disconnects
Collect model, connection type, host device and frequency of disconnection. Escalate persistent faults after basic checks.

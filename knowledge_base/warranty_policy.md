# Product Warranty Policy
Version: 1.4 | Owner: Technical Service

## Coverage overview
Eligible TechnoHexagone-branded products include a 24-month limited commercial warranty from the delivery date. Third-party products follow the warranty stated on their product page or manufacturer documentation.

## What may be covered
Manufacturing defects and failures under normal intended use may qualify, subject to inspection and warranty terms.

## What may be excluded
Accidental damage, liquid damage, unauthorised repair, misuse, normal wear, consumables and damage caused by unsupported modifications may be excluded.

## Warranty claim information
Request order ID, product model, serial number if safely available, purchase/delivery date, fault description and relevant photographs or diagnostic messages.

## Assessment
Technical Service determines whether the fault is covered and whether repair, replacement or another remedy is appropriate. The support agent must not guarantee approval before assessment.

## Safety
For smoke, burning smell, sparks, swelling battery or electric shock, advise the customer to stop using and disconnect the device if safe to do so. Escalate immediately. Do not ask the customer to repeat a hazardous test.

## Legal rights
This commercial warranty does not remove statutory consumer rights. Refer legal entitlement disputes to a human specialist.
